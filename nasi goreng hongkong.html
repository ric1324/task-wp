Instructions

  1. Sauté garlic & chicken: Heat a little oil or margarine in a wok, then sauté minced garlic and chopped onion until fragrant. Add diced chicken fillet and cook until it changes color and is cooked through.
  2. Scramble the eggs: Push the chicken to the side of the wok, crack in the eggs on the other side, and scramble until set.
  3. Add veggies & rice: Add diced carrots, sweet corn, and green beans, then stir briefly. Add cold white rice and stir-fry quickly over high heat to prevent the rice from clumping.
  4. Add seasonings: Pour in oyster sauce, light soy sauce, fish sauce, salt, mushroom broth powder, and white pepper. Stir well until all seasonings are evenly distributed.
  5. Finishing: Add sesame oil and sliced green onions right before turning off the heat. Stir quickly, adjust taste to preference, then transfer to a plate and serve warm.
