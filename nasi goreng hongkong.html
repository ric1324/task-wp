Hong Kong Fried Rice

15 Mins | Easy | 1-2 Servings

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Saute garlic & chicken: Saute garlic and onion until fragrant, then cook diced chicken until done.
  2. Scramble eggs: Push chicken aside, crack eggs into the pan, and scramble until set.
  3. Add veggies & rice: Add mixed vegetables and cold rice, stir-fry quickly over high heat.
  4. Add seasonings: Pour in oyster sauce, soy sauce, fish sauce, salt, pepper, and broth powder. Mix well.
  5. Finishing: Drizzle sesame oil and add sliced green onions right before serving.
