Instructions

  1. Sauté seafood: Heat a little oil in a wok, stir fry the shrimp and squid for 1 to 2 minutes until color changes. Remove and set aside.
  2. Sauté aromatics: In the same wok, stir fry minced garlic and chopped onion until fragrant and soft.
  3. Scramble the eggs: Push aromatics to the side of the wok, pour beaten eggs into the empty space, and scramble until set.
  4. Add rice and sauces: Add cold white rice, then pour in oyster sauce, light soy sauce, fish sauce, white pepper, and salt. Stir thoroughly over high heat.
  5. Combine seafood: Readd the stir fried shrimp and squid back into the wok, add green onions and sesame oil. Stir quickly for 1 minute.
  6. Serve: Transfer to a plate and serve hot with lemon or lime wedges on the side.
