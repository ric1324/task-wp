Seafood Fried Rice

15 Mins | Easy | 1-2 Servings

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Saute seafood: Saute garlic, then add shrimp and squid until cooked.
  2. Scramble eggs: Push seafood aside, crack eggs into the pan, and scramble.
  3. Add rice & seasonings: Add cold rice, oyster sauce, soy sauce, salt, and pepper. Stir-fry over high heat.
  4. Finishing: Drizzle sesame oil and add green onions right before serving.
