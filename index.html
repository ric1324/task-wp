Instructions

  1. Grind the spice paste: Grind garlic, shrimp paste, and salt until smooth. Add shallots and chilies, then coarsely grind them together.
  2. Sauté the spices: Heat oil in a wok or pan, then sauté the ground spice paste until fragrant and cooked through.
  3. Scramble the eggs: Push the spices to one side of the pan, crack in the eggs on the other side, and scramble until fully cooked.
  4. Add the rice: Add the cold rice, mix well with the spices and eggs over high heat until thoroughly combined and well-heated.
  5. Season: Add mushroom seasoning powder and white pepper. Adjust taste to preference, then stir-fry until slightly dry with a subtle smoky aroma.
  6. Serve: Transfer to a plate and serve hot with side garnishes such as cucumber slices or fried shallots.
